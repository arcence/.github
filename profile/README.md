### What Arcence Is

**Arcence** is a research-first AI company working on **original neural architectures**, **training & learning algorithms**, **mechanistic interpretability, causal verification & model repair**, and **AI systems engineering**—while building the operating system for teams that want to build, verify, and own their intelligence:

1. **Arcence Research Lab** — First-principles research across neural architectures, training dynamics, mechanistic interpretability, causal circuit repair, efficiency, and calibrated reliability.
2. **Arcence Research OS** — Converts a scientific or architectural inquiry into a formal, testable specification (`Atlas → Spec → Proof → Compile → Studio → Trials → Circuit → Guard → Evidence`).
3. **Arcence Foundry OS** — The customer-facing operating system (`Guided Autopilot ↔ Expert Workbench`) that turns task requirements, data, and deployment constraints into verified models you control (`Define → Choose → Plan → Train/Adapt → Test → Assure → Deploy → Improve`).
4. **The Assurance Plane** — Cross-cutting license & IP provenance, privacy/secret hygiene, adversarial red-teaming, cryptographic AI Bills of Materials (`AIBOM`), and one-click rollback.
5. **Marketplace & Customer Learning Vault** — Public datasets and open-weight models at no added catalog charge, paired with Arcence-created models/recipes and an encrypted, single-tenant **Customer Learning Vault** where institutional data, evaluations, and feedback compound across model generations.

### How We Work

- **I · Train less, measure more** — We evaluate zero-shot and retrieval baselines before scheduling weight updates. Every training stage must earn its place against a simpler alternative.
- **II · Claims carry their conditions** — Every improvement claim names its dataset version, split, leakage audit, parameter-matched baseline, metric, seed count, and compute cost.
- **III · Falsifiers are written first** — The conditions that would prove an architecture hypothesis wrong are fixed before any model is trained, so they cannot be renegotiated afterwards.
- **IV · Negative results are published** — They appear in the same ledger as positive ones. A clean negative result on a rigorous benchmark is a real scientific contribution.
