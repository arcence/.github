### What We Build

Most organizations today are forced to rent closed, black-box AI models they do not own and cannot inspect.

**Arcence** is building the operating system that lets any team **build, understand, and own its own AI**—combining guided model engineering with **glass-box science** that looks inside how neural networks think and fixes fragile shortcuts before deployment.

| System | Role | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Foundry OS** | *Build & Own Your AI* | Guides you from raw data and business goals to a verified model running in the cloud, your private VPC, your own servers, or 100% offline. Tests simple search/prompting first so you never waste money on unnecessary GPU training. |
| **Research OS** | *Glass-Box AI & New Architectures* | Designs smaller, faster neural architectures and uses **mechanistic interpretability** to look inside the model's wiring—isolating real reasoning from lucky guesses and surgically repairing bad shortcuts. |
| **Learning Vault & Commons** | *Compounding Company Memory* | Protects your private data, domain tests, and human feedback in an encrypted single-tenant vault so you can upgrade to future AI generations in hours—paired with open-source models and data at **0% catalog markup**. |
| **The Assurance Plane** | *Built-In Trust & Safety* | Automatically checks commercial licensing, scrubs private PII/secrets, stress-tests against attacks, and ships every model with a signed audit receipt (`AIBOM`) and one-click rollback. |

### Etymology · `ἀρχή`

- ***arche*** (*Greek*): The first principle—the origin everything else is built from.
- ***-ence*** (*Latin*): A state of being, as *scientia* became *science*.
- ***arch*** (*Structure*): A stone arch in which every piece is held in place by the others, locked by a central keystone.

> **Arcence**: *the state of holding to first principles.*
